No-equipment workouts: push, pull and full body

No gym and no gear? These three workouts need nothing but a little floor space, a sturdy chair and a doorway. They are built into Progressive Overload, which is free and has no login.

What you need

  • Floor space.
  • A sturdy chair or couch edge for chair dips.
  • A sturdy doorframe for doorway rows.

Push: Chest, Shoulders & Triceps

Pushing moves for chest, shoulders and triceps, plus a squat and a plank variation.

Push-Up3 sets of as many reps as you can
Body straight like a plank, chest to an inch off the floor. Too hard? Hands on a bench.
Pike Push-Up3 sets of 6 to 12 reps
Hips high in an upside-down V, lower the top of your head toward the floor, press up. Bodyweight shoulder press.
Chair Dip3 sets of 8 to 15 reps
Hands on a sturdy chair or couch edge behind you, bend the elbows straight back, press up. Keep your shoulders down.
Diamond Push-Up3 sets of as many reps as you can
Hands together under your chest forming a triangle. Same strict body line - this one hammers triceps.
Air Squat3 sets of 15 to 25 reps
Feet shoulder-width, arms out for balance. Sit back and down until thighs are parallel, drive up through your heels.
Plank Shoulder Tap3 sets of 10 to 16 reps per side
High plank, tap one hand to the opposite shoulder. The goal is hips that don't rock at all.

Pull: Back & Posterior Chain

Back and the back of your body, with no equipment.

Doorway Row3 sets of 10 to 15 reps
Grip both sides of a doorframe, walk your feet forward and lean back, then pull your chest to the frame. The only real pull with zero gear.
Prone Y-T-W Raise3 sets of 8 to 10 reps each
Face down, lift your arms off the floor in a Y, then a T, then a W. Slow and small - this is upper-back endurance, not strength.
Superman3 sets of 10 to 12 reps
Face down, lift arms and legs off the floor together, pause, lower.
Single-Leg Glute Bridge3 sets of 8 to 15 reps per leg
Same bridge, one foot off the floor. Keep your hips level - don't let one side dip.
Bird Dog3 sets of 8 to 10 reps per side
On all fours, extend opposite arm and leg, pause, return. Hips stay level.
Hollow Hold3 sets of 15 to 30 seconds
On your back, arms and legs raised, lower back pressed into the floor. Banana shape.

Total body: Full Body

The workout the app suggests for beginners: six simple moves for your whole body.

Air Squat3 sets of 15 to 25 reps
Feet shoulder-width, arms out for balance. Sit back and down until thighs are parallel, drive up through your heels.
Push-Up3 sets of as many reps as you can
Body straight like a plank, chest to an inch off the floor. Too hard? Hands on a bench.
Doorway Row3 sets of 10 to 15 reps
Grip both sides of a doorframe, walk your feet forward and lean back, then pull your chest to the frame. The only real pull with zero gear.
Bodyweight Lunge3 sets of 10 to 15 reps per leg
Hands on hips, step back, drop the back knee toward the floor, push up through the front leg.
Single-Leg Glute Bridge3 sets of 8 to 15 reps per leg
Same bridge, one foot off the floor. Keep your hips level - don't let one side dip.
Plank3 sets of 30 to 60 seconds
Forearms down, straight line head to heels, glutes tight. No sagging hips.

Getting stronger without weights

Each exercise has a target, like 3 sets of 10 to 15 reps. Log your reps every time. The app shows what you did last time, so your goal is simple: beat it by a rep or two. When you can reach the top of the range on every set, move to a harder version, such as diamond push-ups or single-leg bridges. Weight hints only show for moves you log in pounds. For moves without weight, you track reps or time instead.

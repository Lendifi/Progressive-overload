Progressive Overload

Double progression, explained in plain words

Double progression is a simple way to get stronger. You have two things to improve: reps and weight. First you add reps. When you have added enough reps, you add weight.

The rule

  1. Pick a rep range for the exercise, like 3 sets of 8 to 12 reps.
  2. Choose a weight you can lift for at least 8 reps on each set.
  3. Each time you train, try to do more reps than last time, up to 12.
  4. When every set reaches 12 reps, add a little weight. Your reps will drop back toward 8 at the new weight.
  5. Repeat.
Example. You do goblet squats for 3 sets of 8 to 12 reps with a 40 lb dumbbell. Week one you get 10, 9 and 8 reps. Next time you get 11, 10 and 9. A few sessions later you get 12, 12 and 12. Now you move up to 45 lb, and start again from about 8 reps.

How Progressive Overload applies it

Every lifting exercise in the app has a rep range. When you log all of your planned sets at the top of that range, the app shows a blue hint under the exercise, like "Try 45 lb next". A short line explains why, for example that you reached 12 reps on all 3 sets last time.

One rep short

If you finish one or two sets only one or two reps under the top of the range, and the other sets reached it, the app shows a hint like "1 more rep on set 2 earns +5 lb". If you were further off than that, it stays quiet so your screen is not cluttered.

Pick your jump: 2.5 lb or 5 lb

Each lifting card has a weight step row with two choices, 2.5 lb and 5 lb. The default is 5 lb. Smaller jumps suit small muscles and lifts like lateral raises. Choose the one that matches the weights you can actually load. The choice is saved for that exercise only.

Good to know

  • The weight hint shows for exercises you log in pounds with a rep range. Bodyweight, timed and max-rep moves have no weight hint, so for those you aim to beat your last reps or time.
  • The app only suggests more weight when you have logged every planned set. If you skip sets, no hint shows.
  • The hint is a suggestion. If a lift felt grindy or your form slipped, stay at the same weight and try again.
  • If a rep range is not right for you, you can change it for any exercise in the app.

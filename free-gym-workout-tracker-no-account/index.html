Progressive Overload

A free gym workout tracker with no account

Progressive Overload is a free workout tracker that works in your phone browser. There is nothing to sign up for, so you can open it and start logging your gym session right away.

Open the free app

What "no account" means here

  • No email, no password, no login.
  • No ads.
  • Your workouts, custom exercises and saved routes are stored on your device.
  • You can save a backup file and restore it on a new phone.

What you can log

For each exercise you log the weight and the reps for every set. Each exercise shows what you did last time. When you reach the top of your rep range on every set, a blue hint tells you what to lift next time. The app has 182 exercises to build from, filtered by the equipment you have.

Keep your data safe

Because there is no account, your history lives on your device. If you clear your browser data or lose the phone, it is gone unless you saved a backup. The app has a Back up my data button and a Share backup button. On an iPhone, Share backup lets you save the file to Files or Notes. After every 10 logged workouts, the app reminds you to back up. To move to a new phone, open the app there and tap Restore from backup.

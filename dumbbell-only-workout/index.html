Dumbbell-only home workout: the A, B, C plan

If all you have is a pair of dumbbells, you can train your whole body with three simple workouts. Progressive Overload has them ready to go: Push Focus (A), Pull + Legs (B) and Full Body (C).

What you need

  • A pair of dumbbells. Adjustable ones are ideal, because you will want to go heavier over time.
  • A bench, a sturdy chair or the floor for pressing and rows.
  • A bench or stair for step-ups in workout C.

Push Focus (A)

Push-focused, with squats and hinges to round it out.

Goblet Squat3 sets of 8 to 12 reps
Dumbbell held at your chest. Squat down, chest up, heels planted, stand back up.
Floor / Bench Press3 sets of 8 to 12 reps
Press dumbbells from chest level straight up, lower with control.
Overhead Press3 sets of 8 to 12 reps
Press from shoulders straight overhead. Squeeze glutes so your lower back doesn't arch.
Romanian Deadlift3 sets of 8 to 12 reps
Push hips BACK with a flat back until the hamstrings stretch, then stand. Hinge, don't squat.
Lateral Raise3 sets of 10 to 15 reps
Raise arms out to shoulder height, lower slowly. Go lighter than you think.
Plank3 sets of 30 to 60 seconds
Forearms down, straight line head to heels, glutes tight. No sagging hips.

Pull + Legs (B)

Pulling and leg work, plus a loaded carry.

Reverse Lunge3 sets of 8 to 12 reps per leg
Step backward, both knees to 90°, push through the front heel to stand.
One-Arm Row3 sets of 8 to 12 reps per arm
Hand on a bench, back flat. Pull the dumbbell to your HIP and squeeze the shoulder blade.
Incline Press3 sets of 8 to 12 reps
On an incline, press up and slightly back over your upper chest.
Biceps Curl3 sets of 8 to 12 reps
Elbows pinned to your sides, curl without swinging. Rocking means it's too heavy.
Overhead Triceps Ext.3 sets of 8 to 12 reps
One dumbbell, both hands, lower it behind your head and extend up. Elbows point forward.
Farmer's Carry3 sets of 30 to 40 steps
Heaviest pair you can hold, stand tall, walk. Grip, core, traps, posture.

Full Body (C)

A little of everything in one session.

Step-Up3 sets of 8 to 10 reps per leg
Drive through the TOP foot on a sturdy bench or stair - don't push off the floor leg.
Bent-Over Row3 sets of 8 to 12 reps
Hinge with a flat back, row both dumbbells to your hips. If the back rounds, go lighter.
Push-Up3 sets of as many reps as you can
Body straight like a plank, chest to an inch off the floor. Too hard? Hands on a bench.
Glute Bridge3 sets of 10 to 15 reps
On your back, feet flat, dumbbell on hips. Squeeze glutes to lift until body is a straight line.
Hammer Curl3 sets of 8 to 12 reps
Palms face each other the whole time. Hits forearms and the deeper arm muscle.
Side Plank3 sets of 20 to 40 seconds per side
Forearm under shoulder, feet stacked, hips lifted into one straight line.

How to run it

One easy way is to do A, B, then C, with a rest day or two between sessions, and start again. The app also adds a short core finisher to each workout. You can swap moves or build your own from the exercise list if something does not suit you.

Know when to add weight

Each lift has a rep range, like 3 sets of 8 to 12 reps. Pick a weight where you can reach at least the bottom of the range with good form. When you reach the top of the range on every set, the app shows a blue hint such as "Try 45 lb next".
